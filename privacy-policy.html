Privacy Policy

Prism Shift: Block Puzzle

Effective date: 16 November 2026 Last updated: 16 November 2026 Android application

This Privacy Policy explains how Denshi Interactive handles information when you use Prism Shift: Block Puzzle (the “Game”). It describes information stored on your device, information processed by advertising and consent services, the Game’s network connectivity check, and the choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible for the Game

Denshi Interactive is the independent game-development and publishing name used by an individual developer based in India. It is not represented as a separately incorporated company. In this Policy, “Denshi Interactive”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the individual developer operating under that name.

This Policy applies to Prism Shift: Block Puzzle, the Android puzzle game published under the Denshi Interactive name.

2. Information we collect directly

The current Game does not require you to create a player account and does not provide player profiles, chat, user-posted content or an account-registration form. The Game does not ask you to submit your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photographs or precise GPS location to play.

We do not operate a game-profile server or a cloud database for player progress in the reviewed project version. We do not receive the local gameplay records described below merely because they are saved on your device.

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive the email address and any information or attachments you choose to include in your message. We use that information to understand and respond to your enquiry and to keep a reasonable record of the correspondence.

3. Information stored locally on your device

The Game stores information in its private application storage so it can preserve your gameplay and preferences. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • scores, personal bests, runs played, lines cleared, combo records and level records;
  • Adventure stage progress, completion status, stars, best moves remaining and unlocked stage progress;
  • Daily Puzzle scores, play counts, challenge dates and reward-claim state;
  • achievement flags, tutorial completion, active-run checkpoints and recovery information;
  • collected in-game power-up counts, reward markers and temporary ad-free entitlement timestamps; and
  • settings such as sound, vibration, spoken labels and graphics quality.

This information is used to restore gameplay, maintain progression, apply your preferences and manage in-game reward and ad-frequency rules. It is stored locally rather than sent to a Denshi Interactive player account or progress server.

Local progress controls: The Game includes a “Reset Local Progress” control. It clears the progress categories identified in the Game’s confirmation screen, including scores, records, achievements, Adventure progress, Daily Puzzle records, collected powers, tutorial progress and unfinished runs. Selected settings, earned ad-free benefits and daily reward claim history are retained. You can also clear the Game’s app storage or uninstall it through Android settings; doing so can remove locally stored Game data.

Local data may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its app storage, reset or replace your device, or experience device or storage failure. The Game’s cloud-save transport is not active in the reviewed project version. Do not treat local saves as an online backup.

4. Advertising services and information processed by Google

The Game is designed to support interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded video advertisements. The reviewed project includes the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Production advertising identifiers are not configured in the reviewed project snapshot, so production ad requests remain disabled until the publisher completes the required AdMob configuration. The data practices below describe the Google advertising SDK functionality intended for the release when production advertising is enabled.

According to Google’s current Android disclosure for the GMA Next-Gen SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following categories for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address: which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • user product interactions: including app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information: including app or SDK performance information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage; and
  • device and account identifiers: including Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with accounts signed in on the device.

Google states that data collected by the GMA Next-Gen SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The availability and use of particular identifiers or data can depend on Android settings, SDK features, consent choices and the advertising configuration. You can review Google’s GMA Next-Gen Google Play data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Google and the advertising demand sources selected for an ad request may process information under their own privacy terms. Advertising availability and the data processed can vary by region, consent status, device settings, SDK version and the providers enabled for the particular release.

5. Consent and privacy choices

The Game integrates Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request updated consent information and present privacy or consent forms when Google indicates that a form is required. Where the relevant privacy-options entry point is required, the Game provides a Privacy Options control in Settings. The form and choices shown can vary depending on your location, applicable requirements and the publisher’s configured privacy messages.

When the Privacy Options form is open, the Game blocks ad requests and ad presentation. After the form is closed, the Game rechecks the consent state and only resumes eligible advertising requests. UMP is a consent-management tool; its use does not mean that every advertising purpose is automatically permitted.

You can also review or reset advertising identifiers and related privacy controls in your Android device settings, where those controls are available. Your choices in device settings and any Google privacy controls can affect advertising functionality.

Google’s documentation explains the UMP consent and privacy-options process.

6. Optional advertising mediation partners

The project source contains optional mediation integrations for AppLovin and ironSource Ads / LevelPlay. In the reviewed project configuration, both integrations and the master mediation release gate are disabled by default. They are not intended to receive ad requests in the default configuration.

If Denshi Interactive enables either partner in a later release, this Policy will be updated before that release to identify the enabled provider(s), describe the applicable data practices and link to the provider privacy information. AppLovin’s information is available in its Privacy Policy. ironSource / Unity Ads information is available through Unity’s privacy policy.

7. Network connectivity check

While the Game is running, it periodically sends a lightweight request to Google’s connectivity-check endpoint at connectivitycheck.gstatic.com. The Game uses the response to determine whether an internet connection is available for online-dependent features such as advertising. The network service receives the device’s IP address and ordinary request/network metadata as part of handling the request. This check does not use GPS or request precise location permission.

8. Cloud saves, accounts and progress transfer

The reviewed project version does not have active Google Play Games sign-in, cloud snapshot upload or cloud snapshot download. Its Cloud Saves screen indicates that setup is pending. A local progress migration feature can generate a text code for you to copy and manually transfer; the Game does not automatically send that code to Denshi Interactive. The migration payload excludes active-run checkpoints, advertising consent, device settings and ad-earned inventory.

If online account or cloud-save functionality is added in a future version, this Policy will be updated to describe the information involved, the providers used and the available controls before that functionality is enabled.

9. Other third parties, payments and analytics

We do not operate a separate player analytics, crash-reporting or account service in the reviewed project source. Google’s advertising SDK may nevertheless process interaction and diagnostic information as described above.

The reviewed project does not contain an in-app purchase or payment-processing feature. If purchases or additional third-party services are introduced, this Policy will be updated to describe the relevant provider and data handling before those features are made available.

10. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay and preference information remains on your device until it is removed by the Game’s reset function, Android app-data controls, app uninstallation or device-management actions. Some reward entitlements and claim markers are intentionally retained when you use Reset Local Progress, as explained above.

We do not hold a central copy of local gameplay progress in a Denshi Interactive server. Information sent to Google or another enabled provider is retained and managed by that provider under its own policies and settings. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain a record of the request and meet applicable obligations.

To ask us about information you have sent directly to us, email denshiinteractive@gmail.com. For information processed by Google advertising services, use the privacy controls and request channels provided by Google.

11. Security

Local Game records are stored in the Android application’s private data area using the platform’s application storage. The Game does not transmit local progression to a Denshi Interactive server. Google states that GMA Next-Gen SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and the security practices of third-party providers are governed by their own documentation.

12. International processing

Denshi Interactive operates from India and the Game is intended for distribution in multiple countries. Google and any provider enabled for a particular release may process information in countries where they or their service providers operate. Their privacy notices describe their processing, safeguards and applicable transfer arrangements.
